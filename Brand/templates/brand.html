Brand system · v1 · generated from the project's own geometry

TrackUnlimited

Dark CAD / control-room. The whole system reads as one technical drawing: a near-black field, thin cyan line work, and signal accents borrowed from the ride-control panel the project is actually building. Nothing here is decorative — the drawing is the product.

02

How the mark is built

The mark is a curvature profile: κ(s) for straight → clothoid → arc → clothoid → straight. Flat, a linear ramp, a plateau at constant curvature, a linear ramp back. That is exactly how TrackUnlimited stores a banked turn, so the logo is a literal picture of the one decision the whole project rests on — curvature is the stored quantity, and continuity is therefore a property of the data rather than something fitted afterwards.

04

Type

One family does everything: a technical monospace. The intended purchase is IBM Plex Mono (open source, SIL OFL) with JetBrains Mono as the alternate; the stack falls back to the system technical mono so nothing ever depends on a webfont fetch. Headlines are the same face at a large size — the system has no display face, deliberately, because drawings do not have one.

DISPLAY / 54 An open-source successor
HEADING / 22 What makes this different
BODY / 15 Curvature varies linearly over arc length.
LABEL / 11 SEGMENT VOCABULARY
VALUE / 20 563.84 m

Rules: labels are uppercase with 0.18em tracking; values are near-zero tracking so digits stay readable; display sizes take slightly negative tracking. Never use italic — a drawing has no italic.

05

Line weights

Three weights, as on a real drawing, and the hierarchy is not negotiable: the subject of the drawing is always the heaviest line on the sheet.

THICK / 3px the track itself
MEDIUM / 1.6px dimension lines · borders · panels
THIN / 1px grid · construction · extension lines
06

Line-work colour

Track colour is never decorative — it encodes the zone, and the same mapping is used in the loading screen, on the site, and across the social pack.

Powered

Chain lift, launch. Anything putting energy in.

Free running

Gravity, friction and drag only.

Brake

Anything taking energy out.

Block-state colour is separate and fixed: CLEAR green, OCCUPIED red, BUFFER amber. Do not reuse these three for anything that is not a block state.

07

Motion

The drawing draws itself

Line work reveals by stroke-dashoffset over 1500 ms, cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1), staggered 60–70 ms per zone. Dimensions, labels and callouts fade in after the geometry has landed — a drawing is never annotated before it is drawn.

Reduced motion

Every animated surface checks prefers-reduced-motion and renders its final state immediately. The loading screen also accepts ?static=1 and ?p=0.42 so an exact frame can be captured without waiting for an animation.

08

Do and don't

Do

  • Draw real data. Every number in this system comes from running the project's own code.
  • Dimension things. A drawing with no dimension is just a picture.
  • Keep the field dark and the ink thin.
  • Let white space be white space — drawings breathe.

Don't

  • No gradients on line work, no glows, no drop shadows, no bevels.
  • No stock coaster photography or 3D renders standing in for the drawing.
  • No real manufacturer names, logos or ride designs — anywhere, ever.
  • Don't invent figures. If it isn't measured, don't print it.
  • Don't recolour the mark. Cyan on dark, or single-colour for one-colour reproduction.
